Graduate the **decomp skills** out of `in-progress/` into the **Engineering** bucket, so they ship in the plugin ([#100](https://github.com/Ballganda/BG-skills/issues/100)). The flow `decomp-setup` → `decomp-start` → `decomp-run` is user-invoked; `decomp-identify`, `decomp-spec-gen`, `decomp-ticket-gen`, `decomp-match`, `decomp-reference`, and `decomp-standards` are model-invoked. Each has a docs page under `docs/engineering/`, and `skill-map` routes to the decompilation flow.
